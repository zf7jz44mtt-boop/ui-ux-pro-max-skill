Questions

Before you join

What does the waitlist actually get me?

A private link 24 hours before the public drop, and a reminder an hour before that window closes. Joining does not reserve a piece — the 400 are still first come, first served inside the early window.

How much is it?

Price is announced with the early link. We would rather tell you once, with the drop, than change a number on this page three times.

What sizes are there?

XS to XXL, cut boxy and true to size. Telling us your size on the waitlist only helps us split the run — it does not hold one for you.

Where does it ship?

From Ibiza to the EU and the UK. Everything leaves within three working days of the drop closing.

Can I return it?

Fourteen days, unworn, tags on. Limited runs are still covered — we just cannot swap a size we no longer have.
